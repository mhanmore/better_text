Advice on the notice provision

A small fixture demonstrating provisions, a long quotation, a call-out, and a side note in one offline file.

Summary

The notice should be served by both permitted methods. This preserves a clear evidential route while avoiding dependence on a disputed interpretation of the electronic-service clause.

Recommended action. Serve by recorded post and by the nominated email address before 16:00 on the final business day.

Analysis

1.The agreement makes time material to service. The operative wording is therefore reproduced in full:

Any notice must be in writing and delivered to the address stated in Schedule 1. A notice sent by electronic mail is received only when the receiving party acknowledges receipt, unless the parties have agreed another method in writing.

Context. In the full format this note would be linked to a precise text anchor and would fall inline when the side rail is unavailable.

2.The acknowledgement condition creates avoidable uncertainty. Dual service is proportionate because neither permitted route prejudices the recipient and the postal route supplies independent proof of dispatch.
